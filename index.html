1. Introduction & Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Advance Share, developed by Anshil Technical (Sarita Yadav), handles information when you use the Android application for peer-to-peer file sharing. We are committed to minimizing data access and keeping your personal files strictly on your devices.

2. Local Peer-to-Peer File Transfers

Advance Share transfers files (photos, videos, documents, audio, archives, and APK packages) directly between sender and receiver devices over local Wi-Fi, Mobile Hotspot, Wi-Fi Direct, or Google Nearby Connections. Your shared files are never uploaded to a cloud server operated by Anshil Technical.

3. Permissions & Why We Request Them

  • Camera (CAMERA): Used solely on-device to scan Advance Share connection QR codes. Camera frames are processed locally in memory and never stored or transmitted.
  • Wi-Fi & Nearby Devices (NEARBY_WIFI_DEVICES, ACCESS_WIFI_STATE, CHANGE_WIFI_STATE, ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION on Android 12 and below): Required by Android to discover nearby devices over Wi-Fi Direct, Network Service Discovery (NSD), and Google Nearby Connections.
  • Bluetooth (BLUETOOTH_SCAN, BLUETOOTH_CONNECT, BLUETOOTH_ADVERTISE): Used by Google Nearby Connections for low-power offline peer discovery and pairing.
  • Zero-Permission Media & File Access: Photos and videos are picked using the Android Photo Picker, and documents/folders are accessed via the Android Storage Access Framework (SAF) without requesting broad external storage permissions.
  • Notifications & Foreground Service (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC): Displays real-time transfer progress and completion alerts during long-running transfers.

4. Advertising & Third-Party SDK Data Collection (Unity Ads)

While Advance Share does not operate user accounts or collect personal profiles, the application integrates the Unity Ads SDK (Unity Project ID: 343daf9f-5586-4bf4-b46d-2efe51bf72a1, Game ID: 800414101) to display non-intrusive banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded advertisements outside of active transfers and QR pairing.

The Unity Ads SDK may automatically collect certain technical and advertising data, including:

  • Device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / AAID),
  • IP address and coarse country/region derived from IP,
  • Device model, OS version, language, and network type,
  • Ad impression, click, and completion diagnostics.

For more details, review the Unity Privacy Policy.

5. Security Measures

  • Cryptographically random 8-digit PINs and expiring 256-bit session tokens per session.
  • Optional AES-256-GCM chunk encryption during socket transfers.
  • SHA-256 file integrity verification on every transferred file.
  • Explicit receiver confirmation when Auto-Accept is disabled.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Transfer history and paired device records are stored locally in an on-device Room SQLite database. You can clear transfer history, forget connected devices, or delete received files at any time directly inside the app or by uninstalling Advance Share.

7. Children's Privacy

Advance Share is not directed toward children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Policy Updates & Contact Information

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect app or SDK changes. Material updates will be published at our official policy page and inside the app.
